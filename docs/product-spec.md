## Section 1 — Tool Summary

**Tool name:** The Corporate — Supplier ESG Engagement

**What it does:** A single public page that explains The Corporate's sustainability strategy and Scope 3 targets to Tier 1 suppliers, explains why they are being contacted and what is being asked of them, and lets each supplier choose between two response paths — submit an existing EcoVadis scorecard, or download the full sustainability questionnaire to complete offline.

**Who uses it:** The Corporate's 500+ Tier 1 suppliers, reached via a shared link with no login required.

**Why it exists:** It is the first, minimum step of The Corporate's Supplier Engagement Programme 2026 (Smart Intake model) — giving suppliers clear context on the company's climate commitments and the "why" behind the outreach, before any data is asked of them. It is being built as a first MVP deliverable for a course the builder is taking, and will be extended with further Smart Intake functionality later.

**Build status:** First build — no prior version.

## Section 8 — Screen and UI Structure

One single page, in English, divided into three sections read top to bottom. Black background, white text, clean and minimal visual style throughout (see Section 10).

### Section A — Our Strategy
- **Purpose:** Give suppliers a clear, concise picture of The Corporate's climate strategy and targets before asking anything of them.
- **What is visible:**
  - A short introductory paragraph (2–3 sentences) in plain language, explaining that the value chain is central to The Corporate's climate strategy and that suppliers are key partners in reaching it.
  - Key figures displayed clearly (as a row of stat callouts or similar): total footprint 690,000 tCO₂e (2023); Scope 3 share of footprint 71% (location-based, 2023); market-based 2023 baseline 675,500 tCO₂e; emissions reduction target -50% by 2030 vs. 2023 baseline; net-zero across the full value chain by 2045; 500+ Tier 1 suppliers engaged.
- **User actions:** Read / scroll only.
- **What happens next:** User continues scrolling to Section B.

### Section B — Why We're Contacting You
- **Purpose:** Explain the rationale for the outreach and what is being asked of the supplier.
- **What is visible:**
  - A short paragraph explaining that because Scope 3 makes up the majority of The Corporate's footprint, and because CSRD requires The Corporate to report value-chain performance from 2026, supplier visibility is essential.
  - A brief, plain-language statement of what is being asked: complete one assessment, once — either by submitting an existing EcoVadis scorecard or by completing The Corporate's sustainability questionnaire.
- **User actions:** Read only.
- **What happens next:** User continues scrolling to Section C.

### Section C — Your Two Options
- **Purpose:** Let the supplier choose their response path.
- **What is visible:**
  - A short prompt: "Do you hold a valid EcoVadis Sustainability Scorecard (issued within the last 12 months)?"
  - Two selectable options: **Yes** / **No**.
  - **If "Yes" is selected:** an upload control appears, allowing the supplier to select a file (their EcoVadis scorecard) from their device. On selecting a file, the page shows a confirmation message ("Received, thank you.") No further fields.
  - **If "No" is selected:** a download link/button appears for the sustainability questionnaire (The_Corporate_Supplier_Questionnaire_2026.xlsx), with a short instruction to complete it and return it by email to supplier.compliance@the-corporate.com (per the Supplier Code of Conduct contact address).
- **User actions:** Select Yes or No; then either select a file to upload, or download the questionnaire file.
- **What happens next:** Nothing further in this MVP — see Section 9 for what the upload does (and does not do) technically.

## Section 9 — Logic and Calculations

No scores, weights, or decision rules. The only logic is a two-way branch:

**Input:** Supplier selects "Yes" or "No" to the EcoVadis question.

**Rule:**
- "Yes" → show file upload control. On file selection, display "Received, thank you." The selected file is read into the browser only — it is **not** uploaded to any server, emailed, or stored anywhere. This is explicitly confirmed as demonstrative-only behaviour for this MVP (see Section 12, Out of Scope, for the real transmission mechanism deferred to a later build).
- "No" → show a download link to the static file The_Corporate_Supplier_Questionnaire_2026.xlsx, plus the return-by-email instruction.

**Output:** No calculated output — only a confirmation message or a download link, depending on the branch taken.

**Edge cases:** If the supplier switches from "Yes" to "No" (or back), the previously shown control/message is replaced by the new branch's content — no state needs to be preserved across the switch.
