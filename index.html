Why We Are Asking

This is driven by regulation, and by ambition.

Scope 3 is 71% of The Corporate's total carbon footprint — location-based, 2023 base year. That footprint sits in the products and services our Tier 1 suppliers provide. We cannot measure it from the inside. We are asking you because the data lives with you.

The European Sustainability Reporting Standards, adopted under the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, require us to report value chain impacts — not estimates. This assessment is how we meet that obligation, and it is built directly on the ESRS topics that apply to our supply base.

Net-Zero by 2045 is a shared target. We are not transferring the burden to you. We are asking for visibility first, then we work the gaps together. Suppliers who engage early shape how the programme develops.

CSRD / ESRS
Regulatory Requirement
The Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive requires disclosure of Scope 3 emissions and value chain sustainability performance. The reporting standard is ESRS. Your responses feed our disclosure directly.
Double Materiality
Impact and Financial Materiality
Our double materiality assessment identifies climate (E1), pollution and PFAS (E2), and water (E3) as the primary supplier topics. Each carries financial exposure — carbon pricing, substance restrictions, and water stress in shared catchments.
Partnership
A Shared Journey
Sustainability performance is part of our procurement assessment. Strong performers are prioritised for joint improvement plans. This is the foundation of a resilient supply partnership, not a compliance exercise.
