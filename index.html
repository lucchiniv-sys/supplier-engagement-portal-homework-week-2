Why We Are Asking

71% of The Corporate's total carbon footprint sits in our value chain — in the products and services our Tier 1 suppliers provide (location-based, 2023 base year). Reaching net-zero by 2045 is not possible without visibility into, and collaboration with, our supply base.

The EU's Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and the European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS) require us to report on value chain impacts starting in 2026. This assessment is the foundation of that reporting, and of the changes it will require from all of us.

This is a shared responsibility, not a compliance exercise. Your data helps us see where the greatest opportunities lie, and helps you see where you stand.
